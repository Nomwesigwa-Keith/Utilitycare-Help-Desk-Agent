Title: Meter reading looks abnormally low
Last Updated: 2026-09-14
---
Content:
An unusually low meter reading compared to your typical usage could suggest the meter is under-recording, which should still be reported even though it doesn't immediately cost you money, since it may indicate a fault that could reverse later. Provide your last 2-3 readings when reporting so the technician has a comparison baseline.
