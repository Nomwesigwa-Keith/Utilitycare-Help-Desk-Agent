Title: Reporting a visible water leak
Last Updated: 2026-09-14
---
Content:
A clearly visible leak — water gushing or pooling from a pipe, fitting, or the ground — should be reported as high priority, especially if it risks property damage or road safety. Include the exact location (e.g. near the gate, along the road, inside the compound) so a repair crew can be dispatched to the right spot.
