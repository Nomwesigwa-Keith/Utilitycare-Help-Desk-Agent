Title: Flickering lights troubleshooting
Last Updated: 2026-09-14
---
Content:
Flickering lights can indicate a loose connection, an overloaded circuit, or an issue with the incoming supply from UtiliCare's network. First check if the flickering happens on all lights in the house or only one room — a single-room issue usually points to household wiring, not a UtiliCare-side fault. If it affects the whole house, report it as a possible supply issue rather than an internal wiring fault.
