Title: Estimated vs actual billing
Last Updated: 2026-09-14
---
Content:
When a meter reading isn't available for a billing cycle, UtiliCare may issue an estimated bill based on past usage, which is corrected once an actual reading is taken. A noticeable jump the month after an estimated bill is often the correction being applied, not a new error.
