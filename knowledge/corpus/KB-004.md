Title: Power outage after a storm
Last Updated: 2026-09-14
---
Content:
Storms can damage overhead lines and transformers, causing outages that may take longer to restore than a routine fault. Do not attempt to touch or go near any fallen power lines — report their location immediately as a safety hazard rather than a normal outage ticket. Standard outages are usually restored within hours; storm damage may take longer as it depends on physical repair crews.
