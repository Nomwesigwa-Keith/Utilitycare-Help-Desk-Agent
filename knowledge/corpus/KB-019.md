Title: Understanding your monthly bill
Last Updated: 2026-09-14
---
Content:
Your UtiliCare bill includes a usage charge based on metered consumption, a fixed service fee, and applicable taxes. The billing period and the previous/current meter readings used for that calculation are listed on the bill. If any of these figures look wrong, note the specific line item when raising a query so it can be checked accurately.
