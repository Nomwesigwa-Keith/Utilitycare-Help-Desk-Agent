Title: Contacting UtiliCare customer support
Last Updated: 2026-09-14
---
Content:
Customers can reach UtiliCare through the help line, the mobile app chat, or a physical service centre visit. Response times vary by channel and issue priority; urgent safety issues (exposed wiring, major leaks) should always be flagged as high priority regardless of the channel used.
