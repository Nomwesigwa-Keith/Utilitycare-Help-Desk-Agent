Title: Underground pipe leak signs
Last Updated: 2026-09-14
---
Content:
Signs of an underground leak include a patch of ground that is unusually wet or soft without recent rain, unexplained low water pressure, or a hissing sound near buried pipework. These should be reported with the approximate location so a survey can confirm whether it is on UtiliCare's supply line or the customer's own connection.
