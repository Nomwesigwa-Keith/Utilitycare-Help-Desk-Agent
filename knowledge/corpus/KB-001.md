Title: What to do during a power outage
Last Updated: 2026-09-14
---
Content:
If your power goes out, first check whether neighbours on your street are also affected — this usually means a grid-level outage rather than a fault specific to your home. Report the outage through the UtiliCare help line or app, including your account number and area name. Avoid using candles near flammable material; use a torch or battery lamp instead. Unplug sensitive electronics such as TVs, computers, and fridges to protect them from a power surge when electricity is restored.
