Title: Meter display not working
Last Updated: 2026-09-14
---
Content:
A blank or non-responsive meter display can mean a power supply issue to the meter itself, a display fault, or in rare cases a fully failed unit. Confirm the property still has electricity before reporting — if power is present but the display shows nothing, this is likely a meter hardware fault requiring a technician visit, not an outage.
