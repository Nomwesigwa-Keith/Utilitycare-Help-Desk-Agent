Title: Reporting a power outage
Last Updated: 2026-09-14
---
Content:
To report a power outage, contact the UtiliCare help desk with your account number, area/zone name, and roughly when the outage started. If it is a widespread outage already logged in the system, the agent will confirm this and give an estimated restoration time rather than opening a duplicate ticket. If no outage is logged for your area, a technician visit may be scheduled.
