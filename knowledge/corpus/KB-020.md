Title: Disputing a billing charge
Last Updated: 2026-09-14
---
Content:
To dispute a charge, specify exactly which line item you believe is incorrect and why — e.g. a reading that doesn't match your own record, or a fee you don't recognise. Disputes are reviewed against your account's meter history before any adjustment is made; this is a human billing-team decision, not something the AI triage assistant can resolve directly.
