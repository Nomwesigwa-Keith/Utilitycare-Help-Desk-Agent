Title: Partial power loss in the home
Last Updated: 2026-09-14
---
Content:
Partial power loss — where some rooms or outlets have electricity and others don't — is usually a household wiring or breaker issue rather than a UtiliCare supply fault, since supply faults typically affect the whole property. Check your home's breaker/fuse box first. If all breakers appear normal and the issue persists, log it as a suspected supply-side partial fault for inspection.
