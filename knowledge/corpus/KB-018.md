Title: Water leak causing high bill
Last Updated: 2026-09-14
---
Content:
An unexplained spike in your water bill, especially combined with any sound of running water when all taps are off, often points to a hidden leak, commonly in aging internal pipework. Reporting this allows a technician to check both your meter accuracy and possible leak points before the next billing cycle.
