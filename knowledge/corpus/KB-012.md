Title: Understanding your meter reading
Last Updated: 2026-09-14
---
Content:
Your meter reading shows cumulative usage, not a per-month figure — your bill is calculated from the difference between two readings. If you're unsure how to read your specific meter type (digital vs analogue dial), the help desk can walk you through it, or you can request a photo-based reading verification.
