Title: Dripping tap troubleshooting
Last Updated: 2026-09-14
---
Content:
A slow, persistent drip from an internal tap is usually a worn washer or fitting inside the home rather than a UtiliCare supply-side fault, and is typically the customer's own plumbing responsibility. However, if the dripping is from an external meter connection or before the property's internal pipework, it should be reported as a possible UtiliCare-side leak.
