Category: meter_fault
Title: Physical damage to meter
Last Updated: 2026-09-14
---
Content:
Visible damage to a meter — cracked casing, burn marks, or a loose mounting — should be reported immediately as a safety issue, not a routine fault. Do not touch a meter that shows signs of burning or scorching. Photograph the damage if it is safe to do so and include this in your report.
