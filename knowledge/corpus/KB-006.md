Title: Frequent short power interruptions
Last Updated: 2026-09-14
---
Content:
Repeated brief power interruptions (a few seconds, several times a day) often indicate an unstable local supply rather than a single outage event. This is different from a single outage report and should be logged as a recurring supply-quality issue, since it usually requires a technician inspection rather than a simple outage restoration.
