Title: Transferring service to a new address
Last Updated: 2026-09-14
---
Content:
When moving, customers should request either a transfer of their existing account or closure of the old account and setup of a new one at the new address, depending on whether they are staying within UtiliCare's service area. A final meter reading at the old address is required to close out billing correctly.
