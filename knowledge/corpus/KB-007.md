Title: Suspected meter running fast
Last Updated: 2026-09-14
---
Content:
If your electricity bill suddenly increases without a corresponding increase in usage, your meter may be running fast. Note your meter reading at a fixed time each day for 3-5 days and compare it with your typical usage pattern before raising a fault ticket — this evidence helps route the case to a technician faster. Do not attempt to open or tamper with the meter yourself.
